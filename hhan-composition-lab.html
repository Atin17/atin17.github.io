HHAN compositional recall lab

Can an associative memory recover what it never stored?

Probe an inspectable, fixed-seed toy model of compositional recall. A noisy cue is generated from a held-out union of stored primitives; the retrieval process must identify a constituent set and reconstruct its union on demand.

Scope: this is a didactic, deterministic mechanism demonstration—not a reproduction of HHAN manuscript benchmarks or a claim of production performance. Its purpose is to make the compositional hypothesis, control conditions, and storage distinction inspectable in a browser.

What the controls isolate
01

Store primitives, not unions

The visual bank contains primitive support maps. Composite candidates are generated transiently only while answering a query.

02

Corrupt a held-out cue

Active support is deleted and irrelevant support is injected. The decoder only receives this noisy query, not its component labels.

03

Ablate relation structure

Aligned and shuffled traces have equal scalar capacity. Changing their mapping tests whether structure, rather than extra context alone, guides the hypothesis.
