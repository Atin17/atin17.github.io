Can associative memory recover a union of handwritten digits it never stored?

What the controls isolate
01

Choose real MNIST primitives

\(P_1,P_2 \in \{0,\ldots,9\}\) select digit classes. The displayed handwritten instances change with the seed; their union is never added to the stored bank.

02

Corrupt the union cue

Target pixels are deleted and background pixels are injected. Candidate scoring receives the resulting cue but never the selected labels.

03

Ablate compact context

Aligned and shuffled modes have equal trace dimensionality. Shuffling disrupts the candidate-to-trace mapping rather than adding or removing information.
